  \item Attrition prediction with ML: most studies train and test on one dataset (mostly IBM) and report Accuracy or F1; leakage risks such as resampling before splitting. \src{docs/phieu\_doc/bang1\_phuong\_phap\_luan.csv, 6c056db}
  \item Transfer for tabular and heterogeneous domains: Levin et al.\ (task transfer within one table), CARTE (column-name graphs, needs similar labels), CORAL, Daum\'e III, TrAdaBoost. \src{docs/phieu\_doc/ [11], [24], [33]}
  \item Tabular foundation model as a strong target-only baseline: TabPFN v2. \src{docs/phieu\_doc/ [25]}
  \item Closest work: Pereira (2025) thesis, TrAdaBoost between two HR datasets; differs in feature space, de-duplication, label budget, missing target-only baseline, evaluation on the training target domain. \src{docs/phieu\_doc/[44], faa0f6b}
  \item Evaluation methodology: corrected resampled $t$-test (Nadeau--Bengio), Holm correction, MDE.

  \item Four public domains (Table~\ref{tab:t1}): rows after de-duplication, positive rate, nature (fictional, real, simulated, censored label). \src{eda/bang1\_tong\_quan\_mien.csv, d197041}

  \item Input $\to$ output $\to$ metric: $k$ labelled target rows plus a labelled source $\to$ attrition score $\to$ PR-AUC (primary; not comparable across domains with different positive rates).
